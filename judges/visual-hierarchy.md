The output is the HTML and CSS of one page. Read the CSS and work out what a viewer sees first.

PASS when the one fact that matters most (the degraded service or the overall state) is the most prominent element after the page title, by size, weight, colour or position, and the remaining items read as one consistent list.

FAIL when every item carries the same visual weight, when decoration outranks the key fact, or when the key fact sits below content of less importance.

Judge nothing else: colour choice and copy belong to other checks.
