The reviewer is Kyle. He picks the glyph he would ship in the product, at 16 to 24 px beside a text label.

Read each SVG and work out what it draws at 16 px. Weigh these, in this order:
1. Illustrative clarity: the glyph reads as the subject with the label hidden.
2. Metaphor fit: the object shown is the one a user of the product already links to the subject.
3. Small-size legibility: no part closes up, touches or vanishes at 16 px.
4. Restraint: every mark carries meaning.
5. Optical balance and craft: centred in the box, even weight, consistent radii, joins and gaps.
6. Family fit: it sits beside Lucide icons with a 2 px round stroke.
7. Distinctiveness: it cannot be taken for another feature of the same product.
8. Colour use: colour carries meaning or is absent.

Answer A or B when one option is clearly the one to ship. Answer BOTH when the reviewer would ship either and the difference is too small to matter at 16 px. Answer NEITHER when both fail a point above badly enough that neither would ship.
Judge only the drawings. How an option was produced is hidden and plays no part.
